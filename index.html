HOW TO PLAY
1. SANDTRIX MODE

Tetrominoes crumble into individual physically simulated sand grains upon touching the ground. Tilt your phone to steer falling pieces and send settled sand sliding across the grid!

OBJECTIVE: Form an unbroken 8-connected line of the SAME COLOR from the left wall ($x=0$) to the right wall ($x=79$) to clear.

2. CLASSIC TETRIS MODE

Authentic 10-column × 17-row rigid block puzzle. Blocks stay completely solid—no sand physics! Clear complete horizontal rows to score Singles, Doubles, Triples, and 4-line TETRIS clears.

3. MYSTERY SANDTRIX MODE

Sand physics combined with mid-air piece shape-shifting, TNT bomb explosive craters, sudden earthquakes, wind gusts, and inverted control chaos!

4. TROLL CLASSIC TETRIS

Traditional solid mino grid hijacked by mid-air shape-shifting, TNT bombs, and a sarcastic on-screen roaster that ruthlessly critiques your gameplay!

ELEMENTAL CHEMICAL REACTIONS
SPECIAL FLUIDS & THERMAL ELEMENTS
WATER Flows and levels out flat. Sand sinks through water. Connecting both edges triggers a TIDAL WAVE, clearing ALL water from the board!
ICE Solid frozen water. Whenever an ice piece moves downward or lands, all touching water instantly flash-freezes into ice! Connects for a FROST SHATTER clear.
FIRE Roaring thermal block:
• Fire on Ice: 100% melts all ice on the board into liquid water!
• Fire on Water: Fire is completely extinguished.
• Fire on Acid: Spawns a rising radioactive TOXIC CLOUD!
• Fire on Sand: Scorches ~1/3 of sand into charcoal ASH powder.
ACID Toxic lime acid that dissolves sand on contact. When mixed with ice or water, it converts into flowing DILUTED ACID.
DILUTED ACID Shimmering aquamarine fluid that pools over sand dunes while gently excavating them. Connects wall-to-wall for a DILUTED ACID CLEANSE!
ASH Charcoal residue produced by burned sand. Cascades like sand and connects edge-to-edge for ASH PURGED clears.
TNT BOMB Flashing explosive charge that detonates an impact crater on landing, clearing trapped dunes or minos.
MOBILE CONTROLS
GYROSCOPE: Tilt phone left/right to steer and slosh sand/water.
TAP PLAYFIELD: Rotate piece clockwise.
SWIPE DOWN: Soft drop.
SWIPE LEFT/RIGHT: Nudge piece sideways.
BUTTONS: HOLD, ROT L, ROT R, DROP, SLAM (instant hard drop), PAUSE.
