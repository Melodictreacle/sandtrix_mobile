HOW TO PLAY
SANDTRIX MODE

Blocks crumble into free-falling sand grains upon landing. Settled sand dunes slide dynamically with your phone's tilt gravity!

OBJECTIVE: Form an uninterrupted line of the SAME COLOR from the left edge to the right edge to trigger a massive sand collapse clear.

BLOCK COLORS & ELEMENTS

SPECIAL FLUIDS & HAZARDS
WATER Crystal ocean blue fluid that flows into crevices and floats blocks. Connect wall-to-wall for a water wave clear!
ACID Radioactive toxic lime substance that steadily eats and dissolves touching sand.
BOMB Demolition explosive charge that blasts open a crater on impact.
TROLL CLASSIC MODE

Classic 10×17 rigid block puzzle. Clear complete horizontal rows to earn multiplier points.

TROLL CALAMITIES: Watch out for spontaneous mid-air shape mutations, wind gusts, seismic tremors, reversed controls, and roasting commentary!

CONTROLS GUIDE
GYROSCOPE: Tilt phone left or right to steer falling pieces smoothly and slosh sand physics.
TAP PLAYFIELD: Rotate piece clockwise.
SWIPE DOWN: Soft drop.
SWIPE HORIZONTAL: Nudge piece left or right.
BUTTONS: LEFT, RIGHT, DROP, SLAM (instant drop), ROT L, ROT R, HOLD, PAUSE.
